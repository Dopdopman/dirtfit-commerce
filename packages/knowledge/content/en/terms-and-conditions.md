## [account-security] Account Registration, OAuth 2.0 & Session Security

- You may register using an email address and password (protected with **Argon2id** hashing) or sign in via **Google OAuth 2.0** (OIDC verified email required).

- You may terminate all active sessions across devices at any time via **Sign out all devices** or manage linked sign-in methods inside `/account`.

## [ergonomics-disclaimer] Technical Disclaimer for 2D Simulator & AI Support

> Important: **All ergonomics metrics generated by `@dirtfit/ergonomics-engine` and responses from the DirtFit AI Support Chatbot are for technical reference only.**

- The 2D Rider Fit Simulator models static/rider suspension sag, seat-width wrap loss, boot sole thickness, and 2D inverse kinematics from factory stock geometry specs (`BikeGeometrySpec`) and standard anthropometric ratios.
- Individual rider flexibility, pelvic width, hip mobility, aftermarket tire profiles, tire pressure, and suspension clicker settings can shift real-world ground reach by several millimeters.
- AI Support responses are grounded in DirtFit policy documents and live catalog data, but do not replace physical inspection, licensed mechanical inspection, or a coached test ride at a DirtFit showroom.
- Always verify physical control of the motorcycle at standstill before riding on public roads or off-road trails.
