Every off-road motorcycle sold through DirtFit Commerce undergoes a documented Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) and baseline suspension setup calibrated to the rider fit snapshot attached to your order.

## [motorcycle-warranty] Motorcycle Warranty Coverage by Category

Because off-road categories operate under vastly different mechanical stresses, warranty terms follow manufacturer competition vs. street-homologated classifications:

| Category                                                                        | Street Legal | Powertrain & Chassis Coverage                          | Scope Highlights                                                                                                                                            |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------: | :----------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dual-Sport & Adventure** (e.g., CRF150L, CRF300L, WR155R, KLX230, Ténéré 700) |     Yes      | **24 months or 20,000 km** (whichever comes first)     | Covers manufacturing defects in engine internals, transmission, ECU/EFI electronics, frame welds, and OEM fork/shock seals (3 months / 3,000 km for seals). |
| **Competition Enduro** (e.g., KTM 350 EXC-F, Husqvarna FE 350)                  |     Yes      | **6 months powertrain manufacturing defect warranty**  | High-output competition engines require strict hour-based oil/valve maintenance logged at authorized service intervals.                                     |
| **Motocross Race-Only** (e.g., Honda CRF250R)                                   |      No      | **30-day factory assembly & startup defect guarantee** | Track-only competition machines carry no road warranty; covers initial factory defects identified prior to track heat cycles.                               |

## [handover-pdi] Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) & Rider Sag Calibration

Before handing over any motorcycle (`PREPARING` → `READY_FOR_HANDOVER`):

1. Technicians inspect fluid levels, spoke torque, chain slack, brake bleed, and battery load.
2. Using your order's `riderFitSnapshotJson`, we set **rear shock preload** toward your weight + gear target sag (`~30%–35%` of travel) and verify **steering stem & fork tube height** (e.g., raising fork tubes `10mm` in the triple clamps when a `-30mm` lowering link is installed).
3. All compatible lowering links, low seats, and bar risers ordered in a bundle are torque-checked with threadlocker where specified by the manufacturer.

## [accessories-returns] 7-Day Returns & Exchanges for Parts & Gear

- **Eligible Items (`ACCESSORY_ONLY` & Bundle Add-ons):** Unused **MX Boots**, apparel, bar risers, handguards, and uninstalled lowering links/low seats in original sealed packaging may be returned or exchanged for a different size within **7 calendar days** of delivery.
- **Condition Requirements:** Items must show zero trail dirt, zero wrench/clamp marks, intact tags, and original invoice/order number (`DF-...`).
- **Installed Workshop Parts:** Lowering links, shock springs, or seats already installed on a motorcycle during PDI cannot be returned as "new" after handover unless defective out of the box.
- Approved refunds are processed back to the original PayOS bank account or showroom transfer within 3–5 business days.

## [warranty-exclusions] Exclusions & Improper Modification Notice

Warranty claims do not cover:

- Crash damage, bent subframes, submerged engines (water ingestion / hydrolock), or burned clutches caused by competition misuse.
- Consumables: tires, inner tubes, brake pads, chain/sprockets, air filters, spark plugs, and grips.
- Non-compatible third-party lowering links or springs installed outside DirtFit's verified `BikeAccessoryCompatibility` matrix that result in swingarm contact or suspension bottoming damage.
